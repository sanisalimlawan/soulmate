About Soul Mate Properties

Property, matched with intention.

Soul Mate Properties is a real estate agency serving Kano and Abuja. We help people buy, sell and rent homes, land and commercial property — with verified listings, honest guidance and a team that stays with you from the first viewing to the final signature.

Why we started

Real estate you can actually trust

Finding the right property in Nigeria too often means chasing unverified adverts, unclear titles and agents who disappear the moment a deposit is paid. It costs people time, money and peace of mind.

We built Soul Mate Properties to do the opposite. We inspect every listing before it goes live, stay transparent about price and documentation, and treat every client — whether renting a first apartment or acquiring a commercial plot — with the same care and diligence.

The name says it plainly: we believe every property has someone it is meant for, and our job is to make that match with honesty on both sides.
